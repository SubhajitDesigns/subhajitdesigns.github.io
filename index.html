About Me

DESIGNING WITH PURPOSE.

Hi, I’m Subhajit Das, a dedicated graphic designer and video editor with over seven years of professional experience. I’m from Medinipur, a small town in West Bengal. After completing my schooling in 2017, financial limitations prevented me from finishing college. Since then, I’ve built my career through self-learning, continuous practice, and real client projects. I’ve collaborated with 50+ brands, helping them shape their visual identity and stand out in competitive markets.
