A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hey, I’m Subhajit Das — a graphic designer and motion designer with 6+ years of professional experience. I started building my career through self-learning, practice and real-world projects.

I’ve worked with 50+ brands, creating visual identities, campaigns, social content, motion graphics and digital experiences. My approach is simple: make the idea clear, then make it impossible to ignore.

I’m constantly experimenting, learning and pushing my visual language forward.
